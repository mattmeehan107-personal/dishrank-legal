# DishRank Terms of Use

*Version 2026-11-18. These match the terms shown in the app.*

## What you can add

Ratings, short tips and dishes you have actually eaten. Keep it about the food.

## Zero tolerance

No hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, spam, fake reviews, or personal information about anyone. Breaking this gets content removed and the account closed.

## Reports

Anyone can report a dish, tip, photo or place. We review every report and remove content that breaks these terms within 24 hours.

## Honest scores

One rating per person per dish. Don’t rate places you own or work for, and don’t rate for pay.

## Photos you add

Your own photo of the dish, no people in focus, and nothing personal in the frame. We check every photo before anyone else can see it; until then only you can. We store photos privately and strip location and camera details from them on your phone before they are sent. We never show your name with a photo.

## Photo license

You keep the copyright in your photos. By posting one, you give DishRank a free, worldwide, non-exclusive license to store, show, resize and crop it in the app and in DishRank’s own promotion. Because approved photos stay on DishRank after you delete your account, this license continues after that, until the photo is removed.

## Removing a photo

To take down a photo you posted, including one from before you deleted your account, email dishrank.support@gmail.com with the dish name and place (a screenshot helps). We remove it within 7 days. We don’t take photos down because a restaurant or anyone else asks; we remove other people’s photos only when they break these terms or the law.

## Your account

You can delete your account any time from the You tab. Your star ratings stay on the dishes with no name attached. Photos we approved stay too, with no name attached; photos still waiting for review, or not approved, are deleted.

## Allergies

Dish tags and filters are a browsing aid, not a guarantee. Always confirm with the restaurant.

*Last updated 2026-11-18.*

DishRank helps you decide what to order at restaurants. This policy explains what we collect
and why. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

## What we collect

- **Account info** — your email address and a password (stored hashed by our auth provider),
  only if you create an account. You can browse without one.
- **Your ratings** — star ratings and optional one-line tips you submit for dishes. Ratings
  feed dish scores shown to all users; tips are tied to your account. If you delete your
  account, ratings remain without any link to you (see below).
- **Dishes you add** — the dish name and category, linked to your account. If you delete your
  account the dish stays, with no creator attached.
- **Dish photos you add** — only when you choose to add one, from your camera or your photo
  library. The app asks for camera access only when you tap Take photo, and choosing from your
  library uses the system picker, so DishRank never sees the rest of your photos. Before a photo
  leaves your phone it is resized and saved as a new image with **no location or camera details**
  (the metadata is stripped). Photos are stored in private storage and linked to your account
  while it exists. **Nobody but you sees a photo until DishRank has reviewed and approved it**;
  photos we don't approve are deleted. Approved photos are shown without your name.
- **Reports, blocks and terms** — when you report content or block another user we store who
  reported or blocked what, so we can review it; when you agree to the terms we store which
  version and when.
- **Feedback you send** — in-app feedback ("Share an idea" / "Report a bug") needs an account.
  We store what you wrote, the app version, your phone's operating system (bug reports also
  note which screen you came from) and your account. Only the DishRank team can read it. If
  you send it by email instead, we receive your email address with it.
- **Usage events** — which screens you view and which dish categories you search (for
  example "pizza"), used to improve the app. We don't record the text you type into search.
- **Preferences** — tastes and allergy/dietary filters you choose stay on your device. We
  record only how many you selected, never which ones.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any photos other than
the ones you choose to add.

## How it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) in the United
States. Access is restricted by row-level security; usage events cannot be read back by app
users, and neither can feedback. Dish photos sit in a private storage bucket: the app gets a
short-lived link only for approved photos (and for your own), so an unapproved photo can't be
opened by anyone else, even with its address.

## Your choices

- Browse without an account.
- Delete your account any time in the app (You → Delete account), or by emailing
  dishrank.support@gmail.com. This erases your account, email, any tips you wrote, and your blocks and terms record. Reports and feedback you sent stay, unlinked from you, so the review trail stays intact. Your
  star ratings stay on the dishes **anonymously** — no longer linked to you or any account —
  so dish scores don't change suddenly when someone leaves. **Approved dish photos also stay,
  anonymously**: the link to your account is removed and they are never shown with a name.
  Photos still waiting for review, or not approved, are deleted with your account.
- Ask us to remove a photo you posted at any time, including one you added before deleting
  your account: email dishrank.support@gmail.com with the dish and place (a screenshot helps).
  We remove it within 7 days.

## Allergies

Dish tags and filters are assistive only. Always confirm allergens with the restaurant.

## Usage analytics

To see whether DishRank is useful, the app records what you do in it, for example "opened
the pizza ranking", "viewed this dish", "rated a dish", "a search found nothing" or "the app
hit an error".

- **An install number.** The first time you open the app it makes a random number and keeps it
  on your phone. It lets us count visitors without an account and tell a returning visitor from
  a new one. It isn't your phone's advertising identifier, it isn't built from anything about
  your phone, and deleting the app erases it. If you sign in, events from that phone are linked
  to your account.
- **What an event contains:** what happened, when, which screen you were on, which dishes and
  restaurants were shown or opened, the app version and your phone's operating system. For a
  search we keep the dish category it matched and how long the search was, not the words you
  typed. One exception: when a search finds nothing and every word in it is a common food or
  dish word from a fixed list built into the app (for example "ramen"), we keep those words, so
  we learn which dishes people want that we don't cover yet. Names, addresses, notes and
  allergy or diet words are never on that list. For an error we keep a short technical
  description with anything you typed removed.
  We never record your allergy or dietary choices, only how many you picked.
- **What we don't do:** no advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites,
  no third-party analytics or advertising companies, and we don't sell or share this data. Only
  the DishRank team can read it.
- **How long:** once a month, events more than 13 months old are **de-identified**: we remove
  the link to your account and the install number, so an old event can no longer be tied to
  you or your phone. The de-identified events (for example "a dish page was viewed on this
  day") are kept to understand how DishRank is used over time.
- **If you delete your account**, past events are unlinked from it. Deleting the app erases the
  install number.

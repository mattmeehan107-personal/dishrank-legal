*Last updated 2026-11-17.*

DishRank helps you decide what to order at restaurants. This policy explains what we collect
and why. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

## What we collect

- **Account info** — your email address and a password (stored hashed by our auth provider),
  only if you create an account. You can browse without one.
- **Your ratings** — star ratings and optional one-line tips you submit for dishes. Ratings
  feed dish scores shown to all users; tips are tied to your account. If you delete your
  account, ratings remain without any link to you (see below).
- **Dishes you add** — the dish name and category, linked to your account. If you delete your
  account the dish stays, with no creator attached.
- **Reports, blocks and terms** — when you report content or block another user we store who
  reported or blocked what, so we can review it; when you agree to the terms we store which
  version and when.
- **Usage events** — which screens you view and which dish categories you search (for
  example "pizza"), used to improve the app. We don't record the text you type into search.
- **Preferences** — tastes and allergy/dietary filters you choose stay on your device. We
  record only how many you selected, never which ones.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

## How it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) in the United
States. Access is restricted by row-level security; usage events cannot be read back by app
users.

## Your choices

- Browse without an account.
- Delete your account any time in the app (You → Delete account), or by emailing
  dishrank.support@gmail.com. This erases your account, email, any tips you wrote, and your blocks and terms record. Reports you filed stay, unlinked from you, so the review trail stays intact. Your
  star ratings stay on the dishes **anonymously** — no longer linked to you or any account —
  so dish scores don't change suddenly when someone leaves.

## Allergies

Dish tags and filters are assistive only. Always confirm allergens with the restaurant.
